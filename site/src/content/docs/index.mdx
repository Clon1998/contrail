<div class="pillars not-content">
  <div>
    <strong>Straight to the logs</strong>
    <p>From profile to a service's live logs in a few keystrokes. No console tabs, no log group hunting: Contrail finds the group in the task definition.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Read-only by design</strong>
    <p>Contrail never changes anything in AWS and never stores credentials. Your SSO session and the AWS SDK do the authentication.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Fast and small</strong>
    <p>Built with Rust and Tauri. Parsing, buffering and search run natively, so 100,000 lines scroll smoothly.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<Showcase eyebrow="Services" title="Everything about a service, on one page">
  <p>
    Pick a service in the sidebar or with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>. Its overview shows the
    running and recently stopped tasks, why they stopped, the deployments in progress, the task
    definition and recent events.
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>Environment variables, with secret-looking values masked.</li>
    <li>Secrets from Secrets Manager or SSM by reference; <strong>Reveal</strong> reads one on click.</li>
    <li>Refreshes every 30 seconds.</li>
  </ul>
  <div slot="media">
    <Screenshot name="overview" alt="A service's overview in Contrail: tasks, deployments and the task definition." />
  </div>
</Showcase>

<Showcase eyebrow="Search" title="Find the one line that matters" flip>
  <p>
    Search the loaded logs as you type: words and phrases, fields, levels, regular expressions.
    Filter the list down or highlight the matches and step through them. Level chips hide what you
    don't need, "Hide lines like this" builds a noise list per service.
  </p>
  <p>
    <code>timeout level&gt;=WARN trace.id:abc123 -healthcheck</code>
  </p>
  <div slot="media">
    <Screenshot name="search" alt="Contrail's log view filtered with a search, the matching words marked." />
  </div>
</Showcase>

<Showcase eyebrow="Tracing" title="Follow one request through your services">
  <p>
    Expand a line with a correlation or trace ID and search the other services of the cluster for
    it. Contrail shows the way it took: the services in order with their last state, every event on
    a timeline, and whether it completed or failed.
  </p>
  <p>Each entry opens that service's logs, filtered to the same ID.</p>
  <div slot="media">
    <Screenshot name="trace" alt="A trace across two services in Contrail, with the events on a timeline." />
  </div>
</Showcase>
